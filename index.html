Open your study tracker

Use the full tracker in your browser after installing the Windows or Mac app. Both views share the records saved on this computer.

Open in browserDownload for Windows or Mac

Your browser may ask to open the installed tracker. Its desktop window stays closed, and the local session stops shortly after all tracker views close.

Quick access: bookmark this page, or drag Open in browser to your bookmarks bar for a direct launcher bookmark.

You can also paste exam-mistake-tracker://browser into your browser. If it does not open, install the latest version. On Windows, use Open in browser in the Start menu; on Mac, open the app from Applications and choose Open in browser.

Your study records stay local. This page does not store them or sync between devices.
